Campaign record for Gordon (Dungeon Master) and KYM (player). The exact region, character name and unrevealed motives remain Gordon's to decide.

## Campaign situation
A young elven wizard (level 1, as discussed at the table) follows Dam, mounted on a brown horse, along a wooded patrol route towards **Outpost 5**. KYM rides a strong black horse whose name has not yet been chosen. Mount and rider are comfortable at speed (movement described as 24 rather than 12 on foot). The mission is to **scout Outpost 5**; that objective is not yet complete.

## Session chronicle
1. A fork in the path separates the riders. The wizard enters a net-and-leaf-covered pitfall. The black horse stumbles and becomes muddy but is unharmed; the rider takes no damage.
2. Three kobolds advance from the undergrowth, with a mysterious silver-haired, glittering-eyed shadow behind them. The player's initiative wins.
3. Two arrows hit (attack rolls 15 and 14; damage 1 and 5). Two kobolds die; the third escapes into the brush.
4. A hostile **Magic Missile** strikes for 3 damage. Dam and the player gallop away for roughly 5–10 minutes, ultimately regaining the route. Their critical observation: kobolds and what appears to be a drow are operating together.
5. After another hour, the companions stop by a meadow and large tree. Edible berries prove elusive. The wizard hunts a squirrel with an attack roll of 17, and the party prepares stew with its travel provisions.
6. Gordon awards recovery of 1 HP from a previous d6 roll and 1 HP after the rest and meal. Their arrival at **Outpost 5** occurs in the evening.
