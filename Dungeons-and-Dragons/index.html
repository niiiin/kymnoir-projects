THE CHRONICLES
OF OUTPOST FIVE

Session I · 20 October 2026 · A chronicle for Gordon, Dungeon Master

Prologue

A young elven wizard rides a nameless black horse through a wooded patrol route beside Dam, his guide on a brown horse. They are bound for Outpost 5, which they have been tasked to scout. The map and exact province have yet to be revealed.

Chapter I · The Hidden Pit

Dam turns left; our wizard rides right, straight into a concealed pitfall covered with leaves and netting. The horse stumbles and sinks partly into the trap. Rider and horse escape injury, but the black horse is streaked with mud.

Chapter II · Two Arrows in the Brambles

Three kobolds advance from the woods, with a glittering-eyed figure whose long hair shines silver in the background. At roughly fifteen metres, the wizard draws a bow. Two attacks land: 15 and 14 on the d20, inflicting 1 and 5 damage. Two kobolds fall; the third flees.

Chapter III · The Dark Spell

A hostile Magic Missile strikes the wizard, inflicting 3 damage. The companions gallop out of danger and eventually regain their route. The terrible new clue is not merely a kobold trap: the creatures seem allied with a drow or other dark figure. Who cast the spell remains unconfirmed.

Chapter IV · Fire, Food and Recovery

After another hour's ride the pair make a rest stop at a tranquil meadow and a sturdy tree. There are no suitable berries to be found. A squirrel falls to a well-aimed shot (17), and a stew is prepared alongside their elvenbread. Recovery awarded by Gordon across this part of the session amounts to 2 HP total. One wound remains. The leather protection is scorched but functional.

Chapter V · Outpost Five Awaits

The riders arrive at Outpost 5 as evening falls. Scouting and briefing the outpost is still the active objective. Are the other approaches trapped? Is the outpost aware of a drow presence? Does the patrol route require reinforcements? These are questions, not invented plot facts. Only Gordon decides what comes next.
